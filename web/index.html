Multiplayer

Play with friends: one of you hosts the match and the others join with a code. There is no server and no account: the browsers connect to each other directly. Up to 8 people can play, and bots fill the rest of the island.

Your room

Invite each friend with a code: send them the invite, they send you back a reply, paste it in and press Connect. They show up in the list above. Start when everybody is in.
